--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * 2-Box ranked priority tiers vs unranked general groups.

  * Read-only AUTO audit logs of past ad-hoc dispatches.

  * Dynamic SMTP settings save and POST /test live email sender.
- backend/src/routes/campaigns.ts:
  * POST /dispatch: Handles dual SMS and Email, scheduledAt timestamp, auto-directory ingestion, and auto-message logging.
- backend/src/routes/mobile.ts:
  * POST /api/mobile/sync: Ingests staged offline dispatches and contacts from Android devices.
