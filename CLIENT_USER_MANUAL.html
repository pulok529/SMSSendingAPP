1. Welcome & How the System Works

Pulse Dispatch is a private SMS and event broadcast solution. It lets you send personalized text messages to your customers directly using your own mobile carrier SIM card plan—saving you costly per-message SMS gateway fees.

💡 The System Has Two Simple Parts:
  1. The Web Dashboard (On Your Computer): Open in any web browser to upload customer spreadsheets, create promotional events, compose message templates, and click "Launch Campaign".
  2. The Pulse Sender App (On Your Android Phone): An app installed on your phone with a SIM card. It securely pulls the messages you queued on the web and sends them out automatically.

2. Logging In to Your Web Dashboard

Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox) and navigate to the web address provided by your service administrator.

1
Enter Your Login Credentials:
• Email Address: Your registered business email (e.g. client@yourbusiness.com)
• Password: Your private password provided by the administrator.
2
Click Sign In. You will be taken directly to your personalized business dashboard.
🔒 Private & Secure: All your customer records, phone numbers, campaigns, and delivery logs are 100% private to your company. No other business can see your data.

3. Step 1: Importing Your Contact List (Excel / CSV)

You can import hundreds or thousands of contacts in seconds using an Excel spreadsheet (.xlsx, .xls) or .csv file.

1
Click Imports in the left sidebar menu.
2
Prepare Your Spreadsheet: Your file should include column headers like:
Name (Required) Mobile (Required) Company City Tags / Category
Sarah Jenkins +61 422 111 222 Apex Retail Sydney VIP, Wholesale
David Miller +61 400 333 444 Metro Cafe Melbourne Regular Client
3
Drag and drop your Excel file into the upload box on screen.
4
Set Default Message: You can type an optional welcome or broadcast message using the [Name] tag (e.g. Hi [Name], welcome to our VIP broadcast!).
5
Click Import & Queue Campaign. The system verifies mobile numbers, removes duplicates, and saves them to your directory.

4. Step 2: Managing Customer Contacts

Click Customers in the sidebar to view and search your contact directory.

👥 Customer Directory Toolbar Search & Filters

🔍 Search Bar: Type any customer name, phone number, company, or city to filter instantly.
🔘 Consent Filters: Filter contacts by All Contacts, SMS Consented, or Email Consented.
➕ + Add Customer Button: Add individual walk-in clients manually.

Adding a Customer Manually:

  • Full Name: Customer's full name (e.g. Michael Smith).
  • Mobile Phone: Mobile number with country code (e.g. +61 412 000 999).
  • Company / City: Optional organization name and city.
  • SMS Consent Checkbox: Ensure "Consents to SMS Broadcasts" is checked so the customer can receive your texts.

5. Step 3: Scheduling Events & Invitations

If you are hosting a sale, conference, VIP night, or webinar, click Events in the sidebar to create scheduled event broadcasts.

1
Click + Create Event at the top right of the page.
2
Fill in the Event Details:
  • Event Title: Name of your event (e.g. Annual VIP Showcase 2026).
  • Event Date & Time: Select the date and starting time.
  • Venue / Location: Venue address or online webinar link (e.g. Grand Ballroom, Sydney).
  • Target Audience: Select who should be invited (e.g. VIP Members or All Customers).
  • SMS Broadcast Message: Write your text message using dynamic tags like:
    Hi {{name}}, you are invited to {{event}} at {{venue}} on {{date}}!
3
Click Create Event.
4
When you are ready to send the invitations, simply click Launch Campaign on the event card!

6. Step 4: Reusable Message Templates & Merge Tags

Click Templates in the sidebar to save frequently used SMS announcements.

Understanding Merge Tags:

Merge tags automatically replace placeholder words with each customer's actual personal information:

Merge Tag What It Inserts Example Output in SMS
{{name}} Customer's full name "Hi Sarah, your offer is ready!"
{{company}} Customer's company name "Special discount for Apex Retail team."
{{event}} Title of the linked event "Reminder: Annual VIP Sale starts soon."
{{venue}} Event location or address "Doors open at Grand Ballroom."
💡 Example Template:
Hi {{name}}, thank you for choosing {{company}}! Show this SMS at our store for an exclusive 20% discount today only.

7. Step 5: Launching SMS Broadcast Campaigns

When you are ready to broadcast text messages to your audience, navigate to Campaigns.

1
Click + Create Campaign.
2
Set Campaign Parameters:
  • Campaign Name: Name for your broadcast (e.g. Weekend Flash Sale SMS).
  • Channel: Select SMS (Android SIM).
  • Linked Event (Optional): Select an event if this is an invitation, or leave as Standalone.
  • Message Content: Customize the text message to be sent.
  • Audience Limit: Enter how many contacts to send to (e.g. 50, 200, or 500).
3
Click Launch & Queue SMS.
4
The messages immediately appear in the Live Android SIM Dispatch Queue at the bottom of the page. Your connected Android phone will pick them up and send them!

8. Step 6: Setting Up Your Android Companion Phone

Your Android smartphone acts as your private SMS broadcast transmitter using its carrier SIM card.

1
Install the APK: Open the provided Pulse Sender.apk installation file on any Android phone (Android 10+) and tap Install.
2
Set the Server Address: Open the app. On the login screen, tap 🌐 API Gateway Server and enter the server address provided by your administrator (e.g. https://your-domain.com or local IP).
3
Log In: Enter your client Email and Password and tap Sign In.
4
Grant Permissions: When prompted on first launch, tap Allow for:
  • SMS Permission: Allows the app to send text messages through your SIM card.
  • Notification Permission: Keeps the app running smoothly in the background.
⚡ Automatic Operation: Once logged in, your phone automatically connects to your web account. Whenever you launch an SMS campaign from your computer, your phone will automatically send out the messages. You can leave the phone on a desk plugged into its charger.

10. Helpful Tips & Frequently Asked Questions

Question / Scenario Recommended Solution
How many SMS can I send per day? Most standard mobile carrier plans allow 500 to 2,000 SMS/day. We recommend sending in batches of 100–300 at a time with a 2-second delay between messages.
Does my phone need an internet connection? Yes. The phone needs Wi-Fi or mobile data to receive message jobs from your web dashboard, and active cellular SIM signal to transmit the SMS.
Can I lock my phone screen while sending? Yes. Pulse Sender runs as an official Android background service. You can lock your phone and let it send in the background.
Android stops sending when idle? Go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Pulse Sender → Battery → Select "Unrestricted" so Android doesn't put the app to sleep.
What if I forget my password? Contact your platform administrator. They can instantly reset your password for you in the Users directory.
